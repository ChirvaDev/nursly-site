What's inside

  • Year at a glance (2027): all 12 months on one page — mark each day with a shift code to see your whole year, vacation and stretches of nights.
  • Monthly pages: a box for every day with space for the shift and paid hours. Weekends are shaded so weekend shifts stand out.
  • Monthly totals: shifts worked, total hours, night and weekend shifts, overtime hours, holiday hours and estimated pay.
  • Shift codes: D = day, N = night, E = evening, O = off, OC = on-call, PTO = paid time off.

How to use the template

When your schedule is posted, write the shift code in each day and the paid hours below it (after unpaid breaks). At the end of the month, add up your shifts and hours and fill in the totals. To estimate your pay with night and weekend differentials and overtime, use the nurse overtime calculator.

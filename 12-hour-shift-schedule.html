How to use this generator

Choose your rotation and the date your cycle starts — the first shift of a block. The calendar repeats the pattern from that day. Change the shift length if you don't work 12-hour shifts, and set the day your pay week starts to see how hours fall into each paycheck. The link updates as you go, so you can bookmark it or send it to your team.

Most 24/7 rotations average 42 hours a week because two 12-hour shifts cover each day across four teams. Hospitals that keep nurses at 36 hours usually use self-scheduling or a fixed three-shift week instead.

Does your rotation earn overtime?

A 42-hour average rarely means 42 hours every week. On Pitman, for example, most pay weeks alternate between 36 and 48 hours — and those extra 8 hours are usually paid as overtime. To see what a week like that pays with your night and weekend differentials, use the nurse overtime calculator.

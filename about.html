About Nursly

A shift calendar that understands nursing pay.

Nursly is an iPhone app for nurses and healthcare shift workers. It brings two things that usually live in different places — your shift schedule and your paycheck — into one simple app.

Most calendars only tell you when you work. Nursly also tells you what each shift is worth. Enter your hourly rate once, add your night and weekend differentials, holiday pay and overtime rules, and Nursly works out your gross pay, an estimated tax and your net take-home after every shift. Overtime is calculated with your differentials included, the way it appears on a real paycheck.

It's built for the way shift work actually happens: 8- and 12-hour days and nights, per diem and on-call, PRN, agency and bank shifts, rotating schedules and travel contracts. Add a shift in two taps from a template, repeat a whole rotation weeks ahead, and keep notes on the unit or ward you covered.

Nursly also helps around the shift itself. The Shift Alarm wakes you up before every shift and rings through Silent Mode and Do Not Disturb on iOS 26.1 and later. Widgets put your next shift on your Home and Lock Screen, and you can share your schedule with family or export a PDF report for payroll.

Whether you're an RN, LPN, CNA, midwife or healthcare assistant — in the US, Canada, the UK or Australia — Nursly works with your currency and your rota. There's no account to create: your shifts and earnings stay on your iPhone.

Your time is valuable. Stop wasting it on math.
